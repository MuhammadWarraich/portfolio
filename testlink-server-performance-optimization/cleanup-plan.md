# Cleanup Plan

## Purpose

The purpose of this cleanup process was to safely reduce unnecessary websites hosted on the testing server.

---

# Cleanup Process

## Step 1

Export list of all hosted testing websites.

---

## Step 2

Export list of all live production websites.

---

## Step 3

Compare both lists.

---

## Step 4

Send comparison list to developer team for confirmation.

---

## Step 5

Take backups before any deletion.

---

## Step 6

Remove only confirmed inactive websites.

---

## Step 7

Monitor server after cleanup.

---

# Important Safety Rule

No website should be removed without confirmation and backup verification.
